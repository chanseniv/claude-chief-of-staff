description: Weekly family planning. Week ahead, open loops decided, meals, pets, connection time, and a couple check-in agenda.
---

Run the weekly household review. Load the `household-chief-of-staff` skill. Aim for a 20-30 minute session, preferably on the same day and time each week (from `HOUSEHOLD.md`).

## 1. Celebrate (2 min)

Read "Done this week" in `tasks.md`. Name the wins specifically, especially the ones that were hard on low-energy days. Then clear the list.

## 2. The week ahead (5 min)

From the calendar (and email or a school-scan skill, if available), for the next 7 days:
- Each day's fixed points, plus who's covering drop-offs, pickups, and activities. Flag conflicts and days with no coverage.
- Kids: spirit days, forms, payments, early releases, birthdays and parties (gift needed?).
- Pets: preventive dates, vet appointments, refills, food running low.
- Anything 2-6 weeks out that needs a step this week (camp sign-ups, well visits, birthday planning, the annual vet exam).

## 3. Decide every open loop (5 min)

For each item in "This week," "Waiting on," and the Parking lot, give your recommendation: **do** (pick a day and a tiny step), **hand off** (to the partner or someone else, as a drafted ask), **park**, or **drop**. Lead with your call. Keep "Now" to 3 or fewer.

## 4. Meals & groceries (5 min, optional)

Suggest a simple plan: about 4-5 dinners, repeat favorites without apology, one "easy night" (leftovers or takeout). Then draft a grocery list grouped by store section. Put the plan in a calendar note or task, if the user wants that. Ordering anything needs explicit approval.

## 5. Connection (3 min)

- Confirm the couple check-in and date night are on the calendar. If not, propose times.
- Pick this week's one-on-one slots on the kid rotation.
- Ask if there's one thing they want to do for themselves this week (exercise, a friend, rest), and schedule it like an appointment.

## 6. Couple check-in agenda (draft for the user to share if they want)

A short agenda for 15-20 minutes together. *(Practical judgment; adjust freely.)*
1. **Appreciations:** one thing each noticed and liked about the other this week.
2. **Logistics:** the coverage gaps and decisions from steps 2-3 (the short version).
3. **Load check:** "Does the split feel fair this week? Anything you need me to take?"
4. **Us:** date night plan; anything on your mind.

## Close

Update `tasks.md` and `routines.md`, write memories for anything durable (a recurring conflict, a new preference), and show what changed. End with the single most important thing for Monday and offer to start it.
