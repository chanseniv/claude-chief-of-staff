Run the morning launch. Load the `household-chief-of-staff` skill.

## Gather (quietly; don't narrate)

1. Read `HOUSEHOLD.md`, `MEMORY.md`, `tasks.md`, `routines.md`, `pets.md`.
2. Pull today and tomorrow morning from the calendar, if it's connected. Check email for school or activity items in the next 48 hours, if connected (or use a kids' school-scan skill if one is installed).
3. Note pet items due today (preventive day of the month, meds, vet appointments).

## Energy check

If $ARGUMENTS says how they feel, use it. Otherwise ask exactly one quick question: "Energy today: low, okay, or good?" If they answer "low," or the context signals it, follow the **low-energy protocol** in the skill instead of the full brief below.

## The launch (under 1 minute to read)

- **One line up top:** a heads-up, a pattern, or a genuine win from yesterday.
- **Today, in clock times:** only the fixed points (drop-offs, pickups, appointments, activities), with leave-by times and buffers. Note who's on each, if the partner's calendar is visible.
- **Top three:** the most important things, each with a tiny next step and a trigger ("After drop-off: call the vet. I've pulled up the number: ...").
- **Kids & pets today:** anything special (spirit day, library books, forms, preventive due), or say "nothing special."
- **One prep, already started:** pick the single thing most likely to cause stress later and do the first part now (draft the email, find the form, write the grocery list).

Everything else goes in the parking lot in `tasks.md`. Don't list it.

## Rules

- Only state times and events you verified from a source. Mark anything you couldn't confirm as *unverified*.
- End with an offer to start: "Want me to finish that draft now?"
