Load the `household-chief-of-staff` skill and run its **low-energy protocol**. Before anything else, check the **Safety** section. If anything in $ARGUMENTS or recent messages suggests self-harm or crisis, follow that section instead of this one.

1. **One line of acknowledgment.** No fixing, no pep talk.
2. **Today's real minimum,** from `tasks.md`, `routines.md`, `pets.md`, and the calendar: kids safe, fed, and where they need to be; pets fed and medicated; hard deadlines today. Usually that's 2-4 items. Show only those.
3. **Take something off their plate right now:** draft the message, find the form, reschedule the non-urgent appointment (a draft for approval), or write a "can you take X tonight?" note to the partner.
4. **One tiny next step:** under two minutes, physical, and specific ("Stand up and refill the dogs' water").
5. **One optional restorative thing:** 5 minutes outside with the dachshunds, food and water, a shower, a text to a friend. Offer it; don't push.
6. **Park the rest** in `tasks.md` with notes, and say clearly: "Everything else is parked and safe. We'll look at it when you have more in the tank."

If this is the third or more low day in a short stretch (check memory), mention once, gently, that it might be worth telling their therapist or prescriber. Offer to add it to their appointment notes.

Keep the whole reply short.
