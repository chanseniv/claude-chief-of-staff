# Routines

> Keep each routine short: a few steps, in order, with a trigger. Put a copy where it's done (fridge, by the door).
> The "minimum" line is the low-energy version. On hard days, the minimum counts as done.

## Morning launch (trigger: <e.g. after coffee>)

1. Check today: `/household-chief-of-staff:today`
2. Meds (as prescribed) + water
3. Pets fed / meds
4. Kids: dressed → breakfast → bags (by-the-door bin) → out by <time>

**Minimum:** kids fed + out the door, pets fed.

## After school (trigger: <pickup>)

1. Snack → unpack folders (forms go in the "Action" tray)
2. Activities / homework
3. Dogs out

**Minimum:** snack, forms in the tray.

## Bedtime (kids) (trigger: <time>)

1. Bath/teeth → PJs → books → lights
2. Bags and clothes for tomorrow by the door

## Evening reset (me) (trigger: <kids down>)

1. `/household-chief-of-staff:reset` (5 minutes)
2. Pets: final feeding / out
3. Phone on charger outside the bedroom *(optional)*

**Minimum:** tomorrow's first thing is written down.

## Weekly anchors

- <day>: weekly planning + couple check-in (`/household-chief-of-staff:week`)
- <day>: groceries / meal plan
- <day>: trash/recycling
- <day>: laundry
- Monthly: litter box deep clean, pet preventives (date: <x>), check filters and smoke detectors

## Connection (scheduled, not leftover)

- Couple: check-in <day/time>; date night <rhythm>
- One-on-one rotation with each kid: <kid A / kid B / kid C, weekly slot>
